# Privacy Policy for GMS Portal

**Last Updated: April 25, 2026**

GMS Portal ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and share information when you use our mobile application and related services.

## 1. Information We Collect

### A. Location Data
To ensure the integrity of our complaint management system, GMS Portal collects precise location data (Geofencing). This is used solely to:
- Verify that complaints are being reported and resolved within authorized site boundaries.
- Provide site-specific context for supervisors and founders.
- **Note**: Location is only tracked while using the app for operational tasks.

### B. Camera and Media
We request access to your camera and photo library to:
- Allow supervisors to upload "Before" and "After" photos as evidence of resolution.
- Allow clients to upload photos describing their complaints.
- Media is stored securely in our private storage bucket.

### C. Personal Information
We collect information provided during account creation:
- Name and Email Address (for authentication).
- Phone Number (for operational communication between supervisors and founders).
- Role (Founder, Supervisor, or Client).

## 2. How We Use Your Information
- **Operational Oversight**: Founders use data to track site health and supervisor efficiency.
- **Accountability**: Location and timestamps ensure that work is performed as reported.
- **Notifications**: We use push tokens to send real-time updates on complaint status.

## 3. Data Sharing and Security
- **Data Isolation**: We use Row Level Security (RLS) to ensure that users only see data related to their assigned company and sites.
- **Third Parties**: We do not sell your data. Data is stored using **Supabase** (PostgreSQL) and **Netlify** (Hosting).
- **Security**: All data is encrypted in transit using SSL/TLS.

## 4. Your Rights
- **Access and Deletion**: You can request to view or delete your account data directly through the app's Profile section.
- **Permissions**: You can revoke Location or Camera permissions at any time through your device settings, though this will limit the app's operational functionality.
