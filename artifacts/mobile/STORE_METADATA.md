## 📝 Full Description
**GMS Portal: Elevating Facility Management with Precision and Transparency.**

Transform how your organization handles maintenance and complaints. GMS Portal provides a tactical bridge between Founders, Supervisors, and Clients, ensuring every issue is resolved with geofenced verification and photographic evidence.

### Key Features for Every Role:

#### 🏛️ For Founders (The Strategic Hub)
- **High-Level Oversight**: Monitor site health across your entire organization with premium analytics.
- **Supervisor Tracking**: Compare resolution rates and efficiency leaderboards.
- **Geofenced Integrity**: Rest easy knowing every report is verified by precise location data.
- **Data Export**: Generate professional CSV reports for deep operational analysis.

#### 👷 For Supervisors (The Operational Center)
- **Urgent Missions**: Prioritize high-impact tasks with a dedicated mission control dashboard.
- **Step-by-Step Resolution**: Follow a clear workflow with 'Before' and 'After' photo evidence.
- **Real-Time Notifications**: Never miss a critical site update.
- **Tactical Dark Mode**: Designed for high visibility in field environments.

#### 👤 For Clients (The Status Room)
- **Instant Reporting**: Raise issues directly from your assigned sites.
- **Live Tracking**: Watch your complaints move from 'Pending' to 'Resolved' in real-time.
- **Mutual Trust**: Access detailed resolution reports and provide satisfaction ratings.
- **Transparency**: Clear visibility into who is handling your facility and when.
